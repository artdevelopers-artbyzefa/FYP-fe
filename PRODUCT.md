# Product

## Register

product

## Users

University faculty, HODs, FYP office staff, administrators, students, and industry supervisors managing Final Year Projects. Users are in task mode — reviewing dashboards, resolving escalations, managing committees, evaluating progress.

## Product Purpose

A centralized FYP management portal connecting all stakeholders across the project lifecycle — from student registration and group formation through proposal defense, phase development, evaluation, and final scoring. Success means reducing administrative overhead, providing real-time oversight, and maintaining academic integrity.

## Brand Personality

Institutional, trustworthy, precise. Confident blue authority with modern SaaS polish. No playfulness — this is academic administration.

## Anti-references

- Over-designed consumer apps with illustrations and playful mascots
- Dark finance dashboards with neon accents
- Crowded, information-dense government portals
- Bootstrap-era heavy borders and flat gray tables

## Design Principles

1. **Task first, decoration last** — every visual element serves a workflow
2. **Hierarchy through whitespace** — let content breathe, use spacing not borders
3. **Blue-only palette** — institutional trust without distracting accents
4. **Consistent vocabulary** — same button, card, table patterns across all portals
5. **Data as UI** — use charts and progress indicators instead of raw numbers

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG-AA minimum. Sufficient contrast on all text, focus-visible rings on interactive elements, reduced motion respected.
